Podcast settings

Choose your script provider, model, and voice provider. On supported Telegram apps, settings and API keys are stored in Telegram's encrypted device SecureStorage. They are sent securely to the bot only when you use them for a podcast; the bot keeps them in memory for that job and does not save them.

SecureStorage is device-specific and does not sync across phones or computers. If this Telegram app does not support SecureStorage, settings may be saved without keys; use the in-chat fallback for keys.
